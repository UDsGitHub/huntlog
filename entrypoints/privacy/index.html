Privacy policy

Draft. This page is a placeholder; the final policy is written before Huntlog is published on the Chrome Web Store.

Your data stays on this device

Huntlog stores the applications you log, the resumes you upload and your settings only in this browser, on this device. It has no account and no server, and it never sends your data anywhere.

Nothing is collected

Huntlog doesn't collect, sell or share any information, and it uses no analytics or tracking.

Reading pages only when you ask

Huntlog reads a web page only when you choose "Add job to Huntlog" or press its shortcut, to fill in the form for you. It can't read pages otherwise.

Deleting your data

Settings → Your data → Delete all data removes everything. Removing the extension removes it too.
