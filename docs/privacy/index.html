Privacy policy

Huntlog, a Chrome extension for tracking job applications. Effective October 18, 2026.

In short

Everything you put in Huntlog stays in your browser, on your device. Huntlog has no account and no server, makes no network requests, and collects, sells or shares nothing. It uses no analytics or tracking.

What Huntlog stores, and where

Huntlog stores what you enter: the applications you log (company, job title, the job posting's address, status and its history, dates, location, work mode, salary, source and notes), the resume files you upload with their names and notes, and your settings. It keeps them in your browser's own storage on this device (IndexedDB and the extension's local storage). Huntlog doesn't sync them anywhere, and nobody else can see them.

Reading web pages only when you ask

When you choose "Add job to Huntlog" from the right-click menu, or press its keyboard shortcut, Huntlog reads the page in that one tab (its address, title, headings and any job details the page publishes) to fill in the form for you. It shows you the result to check, and saves nothing until you save. Huntlog can't read pages at any other time, and it never sees your browsing history.

No network

Huntlog never sends anything over the network: it has no server to send to. Opening a job posting from Huntlog opens it in a tab, like any link.

Backups and exports

If you export a backup or a spreadsheet, the file is saved where your browser saves downloads, and what happens to it after that is up to you. Importing reads only the file you pick.

Permissions

  • Side panel: Huntlog's window opens beside the job posting.
  • Storage and unlimited storage: your applications, resume files and settings are kept on your device.
  • Context menus: the "Add job to Huntlog" right-click item.
  • Active tab and scripting: reading the page you're on, only when you choose "Add job to Huntlog" or press its shortcut.

Deleting your data

You can delete any application or resume in Huntlog, or everything at once with Settings → Your data → Delete all data. Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything too.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date. Since your data never leaves your device, a change can't affect what you've already stored.
